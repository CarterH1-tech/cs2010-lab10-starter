## Title: “Santa Was Unwrapped”

### Music Description
A bright, mid-tempo Christmas song with a sneaky, grumpy edge. It should start with sparkling sleigh bells and a bouncy piano riff, then build into a stomp-and-clap rhythm that feels like Santa marching around the living room. Verses are half-spoken and punchy, while the chorus is big, catchy, and almost defiant. Add handclaps, tambourine, a low horn stab, and a final gang-vocal “NOOO!” on the last chorus for a theatrical finish.

---

### Lyrics

I flew across the midnight snow,  
With tinsel on my coat,  
I checked the list and crossed the road,  
To make your Christmas float.  
I wrapped it up with ribbon bows,  
I polished every star,  
Then I came down the chimney flue,  
And heard you yell from afar.
